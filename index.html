Before you send it

Three checks that prevent expensive guesses.

A calculator organizes assumptions. It cannot inspect the home or promise the job will go as planned.

1

Count total labor

Two cleaners working two hours is four labor hours. Under-counting team time makes a quote look healthier than it is.

2

Write the scope

Separate standard work from ovens, refrigerators, interior windows, pet hair, heavy buildup, and other time-changing extras.

3

Review the result

Compare the estimate with the property condition, your real completion times, your local market, and the minimum price you can accept.
