Privacy

A small tool with a small data footprint.

Calculator entries

The Cleaning Quote Reality Check performs its calculations in your browser. It does not send the numbers you enter to CalmDeskKits. Your latest entries may be stored in your browser’s local storage so they remain available on the same device; clearing site data removes them.

External links

Links to Etsy open Etsy’s website. Etsy may collect information under its own privacy policy. The links include campaign labels so Etsy may identify that a visit came from this free tool.

Hosting logs

The hosting provider may process basic technical request data, such as IP address, browser information, and requested page, for delivery, reliability, and security. This site does not currently use advertising cookies or a separate visitor analytics script.

Contact

For product or download support, use the contact option in the CalmDeskKits Etsy shop. Do not include payment-card numbers, passwords, or door codes in a message.
